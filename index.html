Questions people ask us

Does the meet & greet really cost nothing?

Yes. Your introduction is free and is not billed to your NDIS plan. You only sign a service agreement if you are happy with your match.

How much will support cost?

Support is paid from your NDIS plan and charged at or below the NDIS price limits. There are no hidden fees and no out-of-pocket costs for funded supports.

Can you support NDIA (agency) managed participants?

Yes. BestCarers is a fully registered NDIS provider, so we can support agency-managed, plan-managed and self-managed participants.

What if I don’t get on with my support worker?

Just let us know. Under our Right-Fit Guarantee we will introduce a new worker within 7 days, with another free meet & greet. No questions asked.

Do I need a support coordinator to contact you?

No. Participants, families and carers can contact us directly. If you do have a coordinator, we are happy to work with them.

Which areas do you cover?

We support participants across Perth, including Armadale, Haynes, Byford, Kelmscott, Gosnells, Rockingham, Kwinana, Baldivis and surrounding suburbs. If you are unsure, ask us.
